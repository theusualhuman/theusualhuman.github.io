Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The Usual Human Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of The Usual Human accounts to upload their own short videos to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

Data the tool accesses

When the owner authorises it, the tool receives access tokens that let it upload videos to the owner's own accounts and read those accounts' name and ID.

Data the tool stores

Those tokens are kept only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect, store or process data about viewers or any other users.

YouTube

YouTube features use the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time from the security settings of their Google, TikTok or Meta account. Deleting the stored tokens removes all data the tool keeps.
